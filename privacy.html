Santino TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Santino TV is made by ByteNero. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect your data

We don't run any servers that receive your data. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you.

What stays on your devices

  • Server details and sign-ins (your Jellyfin address, username and access token; Jellyseerr and Trakt keys; playlist links; PINs) are stored in your device's Keychain. Passwords are sent only to your own server, to sign in.
  • Settings, watchlist, favourite channels and sign-ins are synced between your own devices through your iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). Only you can see them; we can't.
  • Downloads and cached files (films, episodes and songs you save; trailers, artwork and TV guides kept to load faster; data shown in widgets and on the Apple TV Top Shelf) are kept on your device until you or the system remove them.
  • Freeze and crash reports that iOS hands to the app are kept on your device only. They aren't sent to us.

Who the app talks to

The app connects only to services you choose, or that it needs to show artwork and information:

  • Your Jellyfin server and, if you add them, your Jellyseerr server and your playlists: to show and play your media. What they record is up to whoever runs them.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): posters, cast, trailers and upcoming titles. Requests include the titles being looked up, not who you are. See themoviedb.org/privacy-policy.
  • OMDb: IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores for the titles you open.
  • Trakt, if you connect it: your watch history, progress and watchlist are sent to your Trakt account. See trakt.tv/privacy.
  • Apple (iTunes and Apple Music) and Deezer: trailer previews, missing album artwork and music charts, looked up by title or artist name.
  • YouTube: when you open a trailer on YouTube.
  • TV guide sources for the free channel services your playlists use.
  • Chromecast / Google: when you cast, your Chromecast receives the link to the video on your own server.
  • ipwho.is: only for server administrators who tap a viewer's location in the server dashboard; it receives that viewer's IP address to show an approximate location.
  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit: to sync your settings and read the app's configuration.

Your local network

With your permission, the app looks on your Wi-Fi for your Jellyfin server and for Chromecasts. Nothing it finds leaves your device.

Notifications

If you turn alerts on, the app checks your own server in the background and tells you about new episodes, films and albums. Notifications are created on your device; no notification service of ours is involved.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Kids Mode limits what's shown and is locked with a parental PIN.
