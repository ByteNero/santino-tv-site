Santino TV

Santino TV is a player for your own Jellyfin media server on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. It doesn't include any films, shows, music or channels.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email tv@santino.one. We usually reply within two working days.

Getting started

  • Have a Jellyfin server? Enter its address on the welcome screens, or let the app find it on your Wi-Fi.
  • Want to look around first? Tap Try the Demo Server to use Jellyfin's public demo.
  • No server? Tap I Don't Have One: Discover, trailers and following shows still work.

Live TV

Live TV plays M3U playlists you add from a service you subscribe to. Santino TV doesn't provide or recommend any channels. A test playlist of Apple's sample streams: test-playlist.m3u.
